E · 从提醒打开（推送 / 通知中心）E · Opened from a reminder (push / notification center)

提醒点开后不再先进通知中心：先切到对应宠物，再打开健康记录页（FR-167）。D-Day / D+3 / D+7 打开预填好的表单；建议类打开表单、日期留空；D-7 / D-1 等只打开页面（A3）。底下的页面都是那只宠物的健康记录页。

A reminder no longer opens the notification center first: it switches to the pet, then opens its Health Record page (FR-167). D-Day / D+3 / D+7 open the pre-filled form; suggestions open the form with the date empty; D-7 / D-1 and similar open only the page (A3). The page behind is always that pet's Health Record page.
