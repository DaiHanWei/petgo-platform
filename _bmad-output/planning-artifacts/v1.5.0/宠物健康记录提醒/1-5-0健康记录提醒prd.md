### 0\.1 Glossary

Every section uses these terms with exactly these meanings\.

- **Health record:** a structured entry the user creates on a pet's Health Record page \(*Catatan kesehatan*\)\. Types: Vaccine, Deworming, Heat, Neuter, Vet visit, Custom\.

- **Consult row:** an archived in\-app consult shown in the health record list\. Read\-only, and not a health record\. It is either a **vet consult** or an **AI triage**\. Only vet consults count as a vet visit \(FR\-164\)\.

- **Record kind:** the matching key for reminders:

    - Vaccine: the vaccine preset, or for "Other" the normalised name\.

    - Deworming: the deworming type\.

    - Custom: the normalised custom name\.

    - Other types: the type itself\.

    - *Normalised* means trimmed, case\-folded and with whitespace collapsed\. An "Other" name equal to a preset label counts as that preset\.

- **Coverage group:** the record kinds that share one Next date, one Overdue tag and one set of reminders\.

    - **F3 and F4** form one group\.

    - **Every other kind is its own group:** Puppy DP, Combo DHPPi\+L, Rabies, each other preset, each Vaccine "Other" name, Internal, External, Flea \+ worm, Heartworm, Legacy deworming, Custom \(one group per name\)\.

    - **Vet visit is the exception:** each Vet visit with a Next date keeps its own Next date, Overdue tag and reminders \(FR\-161\)\.

- **Why two relations** \(design note, 2026\-11\-03\): a coverage group is two\-way \(F3 and F4 replace each other\)\. "Covers" is one\-way because protection is one\-way\. A Flea \+ worm product treats worms *and* fleas, so it satisfies Internal and External\. An Internal dewormer does nothing against fleas, so it can't satisfy Flea \+ worm\. Likewise Combo DHPPi\+L contains everything in Puppy DP, but not the reverse\. The first draft mixed the two in one list; they are now kept apart\.

- **Covers:** some kinds also satisfy another group, **in one direction only**\. The covered group gets no new Next date from it\.

    - **Covered state:** a group is *covered* while a covering record has an event date **on or after** the group's latest record\. A covered group has no pending reminders, shows no "Next:" and no Overdue\. This is a state, not a one\-off cancellation: every rebuild and every display checks it, so a rebuild of the covered group never brings its reminders back\.

    - The group stops being covered when it gets a newer record of its own \(it starts a new cycle\), or when the covering record is deleted or edited so that it no longer qualifies\. In the second case, its **future** push points and its Next/Overdue display come back\.

    - For the Internal \+ External pair, the pair's event date is the later of the two\.

    - Combo vaccine covers Puppy DP\.

    - Flea \+ worm covers Internal and External\.

    - An Internal and an External logged within 3 days of each other together cover Flea \+ worm\.

    - Any typed Deworming covers Legacy deworming \(FR\-168\)\.

    - The reverse never applies\. Logging Puppy DP leaves Combo vaccine reminders alone, and an Internal alone leaves Flea \+ worm reminders alone\.

- **"Combo" vs "Flea \+ worm"** \(decision 2026\-11\-03\): **Combo** always means the dog vaccine **Combo DHPPi\+L** \(ID "Vaksin Combo", as in V1\.1\.0\)\. The deworming type that treats worms and fleas/ticks in one product is **Flea \+ worm** \(ID label **"Kutu \+ cacing"**, approved by Albar 2026\-11\-03\)\. The research doc calls it "Combo"; the name was changed here so the two never share a word\.

    - In code and analytics: `vaccine_preset = COMBO_DHPPI_L`, `deworm_type = FLEA_WORM`, reminder kind `DEWORM_FLEA_WORM_D_DAY` \(its D\+3 uses `DEWORM_EXTERNAL_D_PLUS_3`\)\.

- **Latest record:** within a coverage group, the record with the latest **event date**\. Ties are broken by creation time\.

- **Next date:** the date the next record of the same coverage group is due\. The system pre\-fills it; the user can edit or clear it\.

- **Reminder:** one planned item for a pet on a date \(a row in `health_reminders`\)\. It reaches the user inside a **notification**\. Every reminder has a **reminder kind** from the catalogue \(Appendix C\)\. There are three sources:

    - **record\-based:** from a Next date

    - **event\-triggered:** from saving a record, e\.g\. the stitch check after a Neuter

    - **profile\-based:** from the pet profile alone

- **Notification:** what the user actually receives: one push plus its inbox item\. It carries one reminder, or several when they merge \(FR\-166\)\. Analytics count user\-facing rates per notification \(§4\)\.

- **Push point:** when a record\-based reminder fires relative to the Next date: D\-7, D\-1, D\-Day, D\+3 or D\+7\.

- **Overdue:** the latest record of a coverage group whose Next date is before today, while the group is not covered \(no covering record dated on or after it\)\. A newer record in the group starts a new cycle\. Heat records never show Overdue\. Backfilled records whose Next date was already past at launch never show Overdue and send no pushes \(FR\-168\)\.

- **Run / next run:** the daily reminder run at 10:00 WIB \(FR\-166\)\. "The next run" after an action is the first run that starts after it\. An action before 10:00 WIB is picked up the same day; one at or after 10:00 is picked up the next day\. A change that happens by itself on a date, such as the pregnant/nursing auto\-clear, counts as happening at 00:00 WIB that day\.

- **Trigger date \(spay/neuter\):** birthday \+ 5 calendar months for cats, \+ 6 for dogs\.

- **Today / dates:** all dates are Asia/Jakarta \(WIB\) calendar dates, computed on the server\.

    - "N days after" means event date \+ N calendar days\.

    - "\+N months" means calendar months with end\-of\-month clamping \(31 Jan \+ 1 month = 28 Feb\), always counted from the record's own event date\.

- **Health profile fields:** the pet profile fields reminders depend on: neuter status, cat in the same home \(dogs\), pregnant/nursing \(females, or sex blank\)\.

- **Heat episode** \(decisions 2026\-11\-03\): one heat, however many days the owner logs\. An episode starts with a Heat record that doesn't belong to an earlier episode\. Every Heat record dated **less than 10 days \(cats\) or 21 days \(dogs\) after the episode's first record** belongs to it\. A record dated later starts a new episode, so episodes can't chain on forever\. Only the first record of an episode counts as "a heat" for every heat rule \(FR\-171\)\.

- **Pet reminders:** the existing push category *Pengingat hewan* \(`PET_REMINDER`\)\. Reminders are added to it\.

## 1\. Goals

1. **Make health records worth keeping\.** Today a health record gives nothing back: there are 1,002 records for 509 pets after a year\. Reminders were planned in V1\.1\.0 but never built\. When a record produces a Next date and a timely nudge, logging pays off\.

2. **Bring owners back to the app\.** Vaccines, dewormers and heat cycles repeat on a schedule, so each reminder is a reason to open TailTopia\. Tapping a due reminder opens the Health Record form, so it ends in a logged record\.

3. **Reach owners who don't log yet\.** Profile\-based reminders reach pets with no records:

    - first vaccine and spay/neuter suggestions for young pets

    - a first\-deworming suggestion for new pets

    - a yearly check\-up for every cat and dog

    - **Note:** adult pets with no records get mostly the check\-up and deworming suggestions in this release\.

**Core metrics:** health records logged per pet · monthly active rate of pet owners · reminder push open rate\. All are compared before vs after launch\. Targets and counter\-metrics are in §5\.

### 1\.1 Key user journeys

- **UJ\-1\. Sari keeps Mochi's kitten series on track\.**

    - Sari adds Mochi, a 5\-week\-old female kitten, with her birthday\. When Mochi turns 6 weeks, Sari gets "Mochi is ready for a first vaccine\!"

    - At 8 weeks she logs F4 dose 1 after the vet\. The form pre\-fills the Next date as \+4 weeks; her vet said the same, so she saves\.

    - Seven days before, she gets "Psst, Mochi's vaccine is 7 days away"\. The tap opens Mochi's Health Record page showing "Next: 12 Dec"\.

    - On the day she gets "Dose 2 for Mochi today"\. The tap switches to Mochi and opens the Add Health Record form with Vaccine → F4 → dose 2 already selected\. She saves in two taps\.

    - **Edge case:** Sari logs dose 2 three days early\. The D\-Day reminder never comes, because the new record is now the latest in the F3/F4 coverage group\.

- **UJ\-2\. Budi's two dogs, one flea product each\.**

    - Budi has two dogs, Rocky and Luna\. He logs External deworming for Rocky with a 3\-month product and for Luna with a 1\-month product\. Each dog gets its own D\-Day reminder on its own date\.

    - Rocky's profile says a cat lives in the home, so the External form shows a permethrin warning\.

    - **Edge case:** one day both dogs are due\. Budi gets two pushes, one per dog, and each opens the right dog's form\.

- **UJ\-3\. Dewi has never logged anything\.**

    - Dewi adds her 3\-month\-old female cat Oyen with a birthday and never opens the Health Record page\.

    - In the next 10:00 run she gets "Oyen is ready for a first vaccine\!" Seven days after adding, she gets "When did Oyen last get a dewormer?" At month 5 she gets the spay suggestion\. She taps it, the form opens preset to Neuter, and she closes it\.

    - A month later Oyen is spayed\. Dewi logs the Neuter on the day of the surgery, which sets neuter status to Neutered and stops all heat reminders\. Day 10 brings the stitch\-check reminder\.

    - **Edge case:** if she had logged the Neuter two weeks after the surgery, day 10 would already be past, so no stitch\-check reminder is sent\.

## 3\. Features and requirements

### 3\.1 Next date on health records \(FR\-158\)

#### Confirmed \(2026\-10\-29\)

- **Which types get a Next date:**

    - Vaccine, Deworming, Vet visit and Custom get an optional **Next date** field\.

    - Heat gets one only for **female dogs that are not neutered**\.

    - Neuter, cat heat and male heat have no Next date\.

    - For species OTHER, the field exists but is never pre\-filled\.

    - **New species** \(decision 2026\-11\-03\): if the V1\.5\.0 species expansion adds species, every species other than CAT and DOG follows the OTHER rules everywhere in this PRD\. That PRD isn't written yet; it should reference this rule\.

- **Pre\-fill:** the system pre\-fills the Next date using the rules in FR\-159, FR\-160, FR\-163, FR\-171 and Appendix B\. The pre\-fill recalculates when a field it depends on changes \(vaccine preset, dose, deworming type, product duration, event date\), unless the user has edited the Next date by hand\.

- **Editing:** the user can edit the Next date to any date after the event date, **up to 3 years after it** \(this covers DHPPi every 3 years\), or clear it\.

- **The vet's date wins\.** The pre\-fill is only a suggestion; the form shows ID **"Pakai tanggal dari dokter hewanmu, ya\."**, EN "Use the date your vet gave you\." \(approved 2026\-11\-03, Albar\)\.

- **Event date:** it still can't be in the future\. The Next date can be in the future or, for back\-dated history, already in the past\.

- **No default:** Vaccine "Other", Custom and Vet visit start with an empty Next date\.

- **In\-app display:**

    - The latest record of each coverage group shows "Next: 12 Nov", unless the group is covered \(glossary\), in which case it shows neither Next nor Overdue\.

    - Once that date is before today, it shows **Overdue** instead\. Overdue clears as soon as a newer record in the coverage group is logged, or a covering record dated on or after it\.

    - Older records show neither\. Vet visits are the exception: each one with a Next date shows its own \(FR\-161\)\.

    - Users with push off see the same display\.

- **Tone:** labels and helper text follow the research's tone: no guilt, the vet decides, and a clear next step\. The Overdue tag reads ID **"Melewati jadwal"**, EN "Past due" \(approved 2026\-11\-03, Albar\)\. "Terlambat" is not used because it sounds like blame\.

- **UI** \(decisions 2026\-11\-04, Albar\):

    - The record card keeps its current layout\. The Next date sits under the title, ID "Berikutnya: 28 Jan"; the form field is "Tanggal berikutnya" \(both approved 2026\-11\-04, Albar\)\. A date in the current year has no year; a date in another year shows it, e\.g\. "Berikutnya: 27 Jan 2028"\.

    - **Record status** \(decision 2026\-11\-04, Albar\): each card shows whether it still needs action, using the Next date and Overdue rules above\. No new copy\.

        - **Overdue:** a red pill with an alert icon, "Melewati jadwal" \(`coralTint` background, `healthEventText` text\)\.

        - **Due soon** \(Next date today or within 7 days, the D\-7 reminder window\): an amber pill with an alarm icon, "Berikutnya: 28 Jan" \(`goldTint`, `tipsBadgeText`\)\.

        - **Coming up** \(Next date more than 7 days away\): a violet pill with a calendar icon, "Berikutnya: …" \(`violet100`, `mint700`\)\.

        - **Done** \(no Next date, replaced or covered by a newer record, or a backfilled Next date before launch\): no pill; the type label turns grey, the title drops to regular weight in the secondary colour, and the icon fades\. Read\-only Consult rows show as done\.

        - Every state has an icon and text, not colour alone\. The same pill shows on Vet visit items in "Konsultasi saya" \(FR\-161\)\.

    - The record's own event date moves from the right\-hand column to the top\-right of the card, next to the type label, in the same "3 Des 2026" format as the category cards \(was `yyyy-MM-dd`\)\. This frees width so titles such as "Vaksin · F4 \(Tetracat\) · Dosis ke\-2" fit on two lines\.

    - In the add/edit form, the Next date field sits under the event date, with the hint under it and a clear \(×\) button\. Its date picker is the same Material picker as the event date\.

    - **Required fields** \(type for Deworming, event date when a suggestion opened the form empty, custom name\): Save stays tappable\. Tapping it with a field missing marks that field red with a message under it\. The custom name, which shows a SnackBar today, switches to the same field error\.

#### Boundaries

- No calendar view, home\-screen card or reminder list in this PRD\.

- Consult rows have no Next date\.

#### Acceptance criteria

- **AC1** For a kitten born 1 Oct 2026, saving F4 dose 1 with event date 3 Dec 2026 \(9 weeks old\) pre\-fills the Next date as 31 Dec 2026 \(\+4 weeks\)\. The user can change it to any date up to 3 Dec 2029\.

- **AC2** The event date picker blocks future dates\. The Next date picker blocks dates on or before the event date and dates more than 3 years after it\.

- **AC3** Clearing the Next date and saving creates no record\-based reminders for that record\.

- **AC4** The latest F4 record with Next 1 Dec shows "Next: 1 Dec" until 1 Dec and Overdue from 2 Dec\. Logging F3 with event date 5 Dec removes Overdue from the F4 record\.

- **AC5** A female cat's Heat form and a male dog's Heat form have no Next date field\. A non\-neutered female dog's Heat form has one\.

### 3\.2 Vaccine preset and dose number \(FR\-159\)

#### Confirmed \(2026\-10\-29\)

- The vaccine name becomes a **preset picker filtered by species**, plus "Other" \(free text, ≤30 chars as today\):

    - **Cat:** F3 \(Tricat\), F4 \(Tetracat\), Rabies, FeLV, Other\.

    - **Dog:** Puppy DP, Combo DHPPi\+L, Rabies, Leptospirosis, Kennel Cough, Canine influenza, Other\.

    - **Species OTHER:** free text only\.

- An optional **Dose** field \(1–5\) appears for series presets\.

- **Series rule** \(decisions 2026\-10\-30 and 2026\-11\-03\)\. There is one rule for every pet, based on the **age group at the event date** and the dose:

    - **Young:** under 6 months old\.

    - **Adult:** 6 months or older, **or no birthday**\. Pets without a birthday are older pets created before birthday became required, so they are treated as adults\.

    - **Series length** \(the doses of the first immunization\):

    |    Preset|    Young|    Adult|
    |---|---|---|
    |    F3 / F4|    3|    **2**|
    |    Combo DHPPi\+L|    3, or **2 if the puppy has a Puppy DP record** \(decision 2026\-11\-03\)|    **2**|
    |    FeLV|    2|    2|
    |    Leptospirosis|    2|    2|
    |    Puppy DP|    2 \(puppy\-only preset; then Combo, below\)|    2|
    |    Rabies, Kennel Cough, Canine influenza|    1|    1|

    - **Next date\.** Rows are checked top to bottom and the first match wins:

    |    Dose on the record|    Next date|
    |---|---|
    |    **Single\-dose preset** \(Rabies, Kennel Cough, Canine influenza; they have no Dose field\)|    **\+1 year**, at any age|
    |    Puppy DP, **dose 2 or blank**|    **\+3 weeks**, due vaccine **Combo DHPPi\+L dose 1** \(below\)\. A DP dose above 2 is treated as dose 2\.|
    |    Below the series length for the age group|    **\+4 weeks** \(next dose of the series\)|
    |    At or above the series length|    **\+1 year** \(yearly booster\)|
    |    Blank, young pet **under 16 weeks** at the event date|    **\+4 weeks** \(most likely mid\-series\)|
    |    Blank, young pet **16 weeks or older** at the event date|    **\+1 year** \(decision 2026\-11\-03: the kitten/puppy series ends at about 16 weeks, so a blank dose there is taken as the last one; this stops a blank\-dose loop of 5–6 prompts before 6 months\)|
    |    Blank, adult pet|    **\+1 year** \(an adult record without a dose is most likely a booster\)|

    - So an adult's **first immunization is 2 doses, 4 weeks apart, then yearly**, whether or not the pet has a birthday\. An adult logging dose 1 gets dose 2 at \+4 weeks; dose 2 gets the booster at \+1 year\.

    - **Crossing 6 months mid\-series:** each dose uses the age group at its own event date\. A kitten that had dose 1 at 5 months and dose 2 at 6½ months is an adult at dose 2, so the series is complete and the next date is \+1 year\.

    - Canine influenza gets a Next date only once recorded \(\+1 year\)\.

    - **Why adults get 2 doses** \(WSAVA 2024 vaccination guidelines, Squires et al\., J Small Anim Pract 2024, PubMed 38568777\):

        - **Dogs:** one modified\-live core dose \(DHPP\) is enough for an adult dog of unknown history\. But the **Leptospira** part needs **two doses 2–4 weeks apart**, then yearly\. In this PRD "Combo" is **DHPPi\+L**, as in V1\.1\.0 and the usual product in Indonesia\. So 2 Combo doses is correct for adult dogs, and standalone Leptospirosis is 2 doses for the same reason\. Cutting adult Combo to 1 dose would leave the dog without Lepto protection\.

        - **Cats:** WSAVA recommends 2 doses of the FPV/FHV/FCV vaccine \(F3/F4\) for adults of unknown history\.

        - The vet's date still wins: the user can change or clear the pre\-filled date\.

- **Due vaccine and due dose:** what the reminder copy, `{vaksin}`, `{dosis}` and the form pre\-fill use \(FR\-167, FR\-169\)\.

    - The branch is the one the series rule gave at the record's event date, even if the user later edited the Next date\.

    - **\+4\-week branch \(series\):** the due vaccine is the same preset and the due dose is the record's dose \+ 1\. With a blank dose, the due dose is blank\.

    - **\+1\-year branch \(booster\), single\-dose presets, "Other":** the due vaccine is the same preset and the due dose is blank\.

    - **Puppy DP dose 2 or blank:** the due vaccine is **Combo DHPPi\+L, dose 1** \(below\)\.

- **Puppy DP** leads into Combo:

    - After DP dose 2 \(or a DP with a blank dose\), the Next date is \+3 weeks, and the due vaccine is Combo dose 1\. Only a DP dose 1 keeps the DP series \(\+4 weeks to DP dose 2\)\. The D\-7 and D\+7 `{vaksin}` show Combo's name, the D\-Day uses the "starting series" copy with `{dosis}` = 1, and the D\-Day/D\+7 form opens Vaccine → Combo → dose 1\.

    - Combo covers Puppy DP: logging Combo ends any pending DP reminders\. Logging DP doesn't touch Combo reminders\.

    - **After DP, Combo is 2 doses** \(decision 2026\-11\-03\): a puppy with any Puppy DP record has a young Combo series length of 2\. Combo dose 1 → \+4 weeks to dose 2, and dose 2 → \+1 year\. This avoids prompting a third Combo dose the vet won't give\.

- **F3 and F4** are one coverage group\. The user picks one, usually on the vet's advice\. The series ends at the last dose; after that only the yearly booster follows \(decision 2026\-10\-29\)\.

- Rabies keeps the regulatory hint from V1\.1\.0 FR\-45A\. V1\.1\.0 set no copy and the app has none; the hint is ID **"Rabies wajib tiap tahun\. Simpan buku vaksinnya\."** \(taken from the approved push copy; approved 2026\-11\-04, Albar\)\. Preset labels: F3 \(Tricat\), F4 \(Tetracat\), Rabies, FeLV, Puppy DP, Combo DHPPi\+L, Leptospirosis, Kennel Cough, Canine influenza, Lainnya; Dose field "Dosis \(opsional\)" \(approved 2026\-11\-04, Albar\)\.

- **UI** \(decisions 2026\-11\-04\): the vaccine name becomes a dropdown with the same label "Nama vaksin"; choosing "Lainnya" shows the free\-text box under it, labelled **"Tulis nama vaksin"** so it differs from the dropdown \(approved 2026\-11\-04, Albar\); species OTHER keeps its single "Nama vaksin" box\. The Dose field \(series presets only\) is a row of chips 1–5, not a dropdown\. Safety and caution hints in the form \(pregnant/nursing, Rabies, and the FR\-160 and FR\-171 hints\) are shown as an amber note with an ⓘ icon, so they read differently from the plain Next\-date hint\.

- Leptospirosis, Kennel Cough and Canine influenza are **never recommended by the system**, because dog lifestyle is excluded\. They get a Next date only once the user records them\.

- **Pregnant or nursing pets:** the Vaccine form shows ID **"Tanya dokter hewan sebelum vaksinasi"**, EN "Ask your vet before vaccinating\." \(ID approved 2026\-11\-03, Hex\)\.

#### Acceptance criteria

- **AC6** A cat's preset list shows F3, F4, Rabies, FeLV and Other\. A dog's shows the dog list\. A pet of species OTHER sees only free text\.

- **AC7** Series rule, F4:

    - Kitten under 6 months: dose 2 → \+4 weeks, dose 3 → \+1 year\. Blank at 8 or 12 weeks old → \+4 weeks; blank at 16 weeks or older → \+1 year\. A kitten logged with blank doses at 8, 12 and 16 weeks gets exactly 2 series prompts, then the booster\.

- **AC9** For a puppy, DP dose 2 on 1 Mar pre\-fills the Next date as 22 Mar \(Combo dose 1\)\. Its D\-7 on 15 Mar names Combo\. Its D\-Day on 22 Mar reads "Dosis ke\-1 untuk \{nama\} hari ini", and tapping it opens Vaccine → Combo DHPPi\+L → dose 1\. Logging Combo on 20 Mar instead cancels the DP reminders\. ### 3\.6 Record\-based reminders \(FR\-163\)

#### Confirmed \(2026\-10\-29\)

Each latest record with a Next date creates reminders at the push points below\.

|Record|Default Next date|Push points|Stops when one of these is logged|
|---|---|---|---|
|Vaccine, preset|Series rule \(FR\-159\)|D\-7, D\-Day, D\+7|The same preset\. F3 and F4 stop each other\. Puppy DP is also stopped by Combo\.|
|Vaccine, Other|None|D\-7, D\-Day, D\+7|The same normalised name|
|Deworming, Internal|By age at event date: under 8 weeks → **\+14 days** \(decision 2026\-11\-03; never a gap under 2 weeks\); 8 weeks to 6 months → \+1 month; 6 months or older → \+3 months|D\-Day, D\+3|Internal or Flea \+ worm|
|Deworming, External|Event date \+ product duration|D\-Day, D\+3|External or Flea \+ worm|
|Deworming, Flea \+ worm|\+1 month|D\-Day, D\+3|Flea \+ worm, or Internal \+ External within 3 days|
|Deworming, Heartworm|\+1 month|D\-Day, D\+3|Heartworm|
|Vet visit|None|D\-1, D\-Day|A Vet visit dated on or after D\-1 \(FR\-161\)|
|Custom|None|D\-Day|Custom with the same normalised name|

- **Heat and Neuter** are covered in FR\-171\.

- **Rebuild:** every create, delete, or edit **of a field reminders depend on** \(event date, type or kind, preset, dose, deworming type, product duration, Next date\) rebuilds the pending reminders of its coverage group from the latest record, and of any group it covers\. A note\-only edit rebuilds nothing, so moved reminders and a pregnancy catch\-up state stay as they are\. A covered group \(glossary\) rebuilds to **no reminders**\. The rebuild is one\-way like coverage: rebuilding Internal alone never touches Flea \+ worm\. The one exception is the pair rule: saving, editing or deleting an Internal or External also rebuilds Flea \+ worm, because together they can cover it\. If a record's type or kind changes, both the old and new groups are rebuilt\. After a deletion, the previous record becomes latest again and its **future** push points are reinstated\. Vet visits rebuild per record \(FR\-161\)\.

- **Other rebuild triggers\.** Only future dates are rebuilt\.

    - **Profile changes:** birthday added or edited, sex, neuter status, pregnant/nursing set to Yes\. Clearing pregnant/nursing doesn't rebuild future points; it follows the resume table in FR\-166\.

    - **A vet consult closing, or a Vet visit record created, re\-dated or deleted:** it moves the check\-up base date \(FR\-164\)\.

    - **Pregnant/nursing catch\-up:** once a catch\-up has been sent \(FR\-166\), that Next date stays "done"\. No later rebuild brings back its D\+7 until a new record or a new Next date is saved\.

    - **A read\-only pet renewed\.**

    - **Event-triggered reminders** \(decision 2026\-11\-03\): when a Neuter or Heat record is created, re\-dated or deleted, the stitch check, pyometra watch, cat 2nd\-heat and male first\-heat reminders are recalculated from the current records, the same way as Next\-date reminders\. Only future points are kept\. A once\-only reminder that was already sent stays sent\.

- **Logged early:** a new record with an event date on or after D\-7 becomes the latest, so the old reminders are replaced\. Records dated earlier than D\-7 replace them the same way when they are the latest by event date\.

- **Back\-dated records:** a push point that falls before the next run after saving is never sent\. The record shows Overdue if its Next date is already past\. This applies to every record at any time, not only at launch\.

- **Pet age:** age comes from the birthday, taken at the event date\. Older pets without a birthday are treated as **adults**: the adult series for vaccines \(FR\-159\) and the adult interval for Internal deworming \(decisions 2026\-10\-29 and 2026\-11\-03\)\.

- **Interval tables:** the rules in Appendix B\.1 are binding\. The research cat and dog maps \(Appendix B\.2, B\.3\) apply only where they agree with §3; their "goes outside", lifestyle and dog\-size rows don't apply \(FR\-162\)\.

#### Acceptance criteria

- **AC18** F4 dose 2 with a Next date of 1 Dec creates reminders on 24 Nov \(D\-7\), 1 Dec \(D\-Day\) and 8 Dec \(D\+7\)\. Logging F4 dated 26 Nov cancels 1 Dec and 8 Dec\.

- **AC19** Internal deworming pre\-fills \+3 months for a pet aged 6 months or more at the event date, and \+1 month for one aged 3–6 months\. A 5\-week\-old gets event date \+ 14 days, and so does a pet aged 7 weeks 6 days \(never a next\-day push\)\.

- **AC20** Deleting the latest record cancels its unsent reminders and reinstates the previous record's future push points\. Editing a Next date moves the unsent points\.

- **AC21** Coverage is one\-way and lasts:

    - An adult dog has Internal on 1 Jan \(Next 1 Apr\)\. Logging Flea \+ worm on 1 Feb \(Next 1 Mar\) cancels the 1 Apr Internal reminders\. The 1 Jan record shows no "Next:" and no Overdue\.

    - Editing the 1 Jan Internal record's note afterwards does **not** bring 1 Apr back\.

    - Deleting the 1 Feb Flea \+ worm brings back the 1 Apr Internal reminders and "Next: 1 Apr"\.

    - Logging Internal on 15 Feb, after the Flea \+ worm, starts a new Internal cycle \(Next 15 May\) and leaves the Flea \+ worm's 1 Mar reminders unchanged\.

- **AC22** On 10 Mar the user logs a Rabies dated 1 Feb of the previous year\. The pre\-fill is 1 Feb this year, which has already passed, so no reminder is sent and the record shows Overdue\.

### 3\.7 Profile\-based reminders \(FR\-164\)

#### Confirmed \(2026\-10\-29\)

These need no health record and apply to CAT and DOG only\.

|Reminder|When|Skip if|
|---|---|---|
|First vaccine suggestion|The pet turns 6 weeks, or is added aged 6 weeks to 6 months \(then in the next run\)|Any Vaccine record exists, or no birthday|
|First deworming suggestion|7 days after the pet is added|Any Deworming record exists|
|Spay / neuter suggestion|The pet reaches its trigger date \(cat: birthday \+ 5 months; dog: \+ 6 months\), **or is added after its trigger date while under 12 months \(then 14 days after adding, e\.g\. a cat added at 8 months, AC29\)**\. **Sex blank** delays it \(below\)|Neuter status Neutered, a Neuter record exists, no birthday, or **12 months or older** on the send date|
|Check\-up|See **Check\-up due date** below|A vet visit in the window \(base date, due date\]|

- **Check\-up due date** \(decision 2026\-11\-03\)\. Senior means cat 10\+ years, dog 7\+ years; the **senior birthday** is the cat's 10th or the dog's 7th birthday\.

    - **Why not "6 months if senior on the due date, else 12":** near the senior birthday that rule has no answer\. Take a cat seen at 9 years 3 months\. With 6 months the due date is at 9 years 9 months, when she isn't senior, so it should be 12\. With 12 months it is at 10 years 3 months, when she is senior, so it should be 6\. The rule below decides the interval from the **base date** only, so it always gives one answer\.

    - Compute it in this order:

        1. **Base date:** the last vet visit\. If there is none since the previous check\-up, the previous check\-up's send date\. Only a check\-up actually sent counts; cycles before launch that were never sent don't\. If there has never been either, the profile creation date\. At launch, a pet whose first due date has passed is handled by the launch queue only, so it never gets a normal check\-up and a queued one\.

        2. **No birthday:** due = base date \+ 12 months \(AC24\)\. Stop\.

        3. **Senior on the base date:** due = base date \+ 6 months\. Stop\.

        4. **Not senior on the base date:** due = the **earliest** of base date \+ 12 months and the senior birthday, but **not earlier than** base date \+ 6 months\.

    - In short, for a pet that isn't senior yet:

        - If the senior birthday is more than 12 months away, the due date is base \+ 12 months\.

        - If it is 6–12 months away, the due date is the senior birthday\.

        - If it is under 6 months away, the due date is base \+ 6 months\.

    - **Copy only, after the date is fixed:** "Check\-up, senior" if the pet is senior on the due date, otherwise "Check\-up, adult"\. This never feeds back into the interval\.

- Each suggestion is sent **once per pet**\. The check\-up repeats each cycle\.

- Spay/neuter copy follows sex: the "spay" copy for females and the "neuter" copy for males\.

- **Late triggers for spay/neuter** \(decision 2026\-11\-03\):

    - **Added after the trigger date:** sent 14 days after the pet is added, if it is still under 12 months\. The 14 days keep it apart from the first vaccine suggestion \(next run\) and the first deworming suggestion \(day 7\), so a new owner isn't hit with three suggestions in the first week\.

    - **Sex blank:** the suggestion waits\. It is sent on the **later** of its normal date \(trigger date, or 14 days after adding\) and the next run after sex is set, if the pet is still under 12 months then\.

    - From 12 months the suggestion is not sent, the same cut\-off as the launch queue\.

- If a check\-up falls on a day the pet gets a birthday, anniversary or first\-birthday milestone push \(e\.g\. the anniversary of profile creation; FR\-166\), it moves to the next day under FR\-166\. This is intended\.

#### Launch rules for existing pets \(decisions 2026\-10\-30 and 2026\-11\-03\)

At launch, each suggestion is sent **once**, only to pets that qualify:

- **First vaccine:** pets aged 6 weeks to 6 months with no Vaccine record\.

- **Spay/neuter:** pets **under 12 months** that are past the trigger date, with sex set\.

- **First deworming:** pets with no Deworming record\.

- **Check\-up:** pets whose check\-up due date \(rule above\) is before the launch date\.

**Who goes in the queue:** only suggestions whose normal date \(6 weeks old, add \+ 7 days, the trigger date or add \+ 14 days, check\-up due date\) is **before** launch day\. A suggestion whose normal date is on or after launch day follows the normal rules\. For example, a pet added 3 days before launch gets its first deworming suggestion on day 7 as usual, not from the queue\. A suggestion is never sent by both\.

**Late updaters: a personal launch** \(decision 2026\-11\-03\)\. For a user who updates to app 1\.5\.0 after launch, the **update day is their own launch day**\. The same rules apply from that date: suggestions whose normal date is before the update day go into that user's own queue, one every 3 days, with a start day spread over the next 7 days\. Later ones follow the normal rules\. Nothing they missed is lost or sent in a burst\.

**Launch queue\.** These suggestions are not sent all on the first run\. Each user's qualifying suggestions, across all of their pets, go into one queue, so an owner never gets a burst on launch day:

- **One launch suggestion per user every 3 days\.** Each user's first one goes out on a start day between launch day and launch \+ 6 days, spread evenly across users \(e\.g\. by user ID\)\.

- **Order within a user's queue:** first vaccine \> spay/neuter \> first deworming \> check\-up, since the first two depend on the pet's age\. Within the same kind, the pet added first goes first\.

- **Real reminders come first\.** On a day when the user already gets a push \(a reminder, a birthday, anniversary or first\-birthday milestone push for any of their pets, or a new\-user recall day, FR\-166\), the launch suggestion waits for the next free day\. It never displaces anything and is **never dropped**: the 3\-day move limit in FR\-166 doesn't apply to the launch queue\.

- **Checked again on the send day\.** If the skip rule now applies \(e\.g\. the owner has logged a Deworming in the meantime, or the pet is now too old for the first vaccine\), the item is removed and the next one moves up\. A first vaccine item is also removed if the pet's pregnant/nursing flag is Yes on the send day \(decision 2026\-11\-03\)\.

- **After the queue:** a check\-up sent from the queue starts the normal cycle from its send date\. Pets that qualify only after launch \(e\.g\. a kitten turning 6 weeks\) follow the normal rules, not the queue\.

- **Pause switch:** the queue can be paused and resumed by config without a release, in case the opt\-out rate \(SM\-C1\) jumps during the launch weeks\.

### 3\.8 Heat and neuter reminders \(FR\-171\)

#### Confirmed \(2026\-10\-29\)

- **Label:** "Menstruation" is shown as **Heat \(Berahi\)** for all species\. The stored type is unchanged\. Spelling *berahi* \(KBBI, as in the app's current "Siklus Berahi"\) decided by Albar 2026\-11\-03 \(UI flow\)\.

- **Who can log heat:** the Heat type is available for every sex and neuter status, because 36% of current heat records are for males\.

    - **Neutered pets:** the form shows ID **"Berahi pasca steril? Tanya dokter hewan"**, EN "Heat after neutering? Ask your vet\." \(approved 2026\-11\-03, Albar; 39 characters; spelling changed to *berahi* by Albar the same day\)\. This deliberately departs from the research, which hides heat for neutered females: owners log *berahi* broadly, and the record is useful to a vet\.

- **Event\-triggered reminders:**

    - **Cat 2nd heat and male first heat** go out in the next 10:00 run after saving, and only if the Heat's event date is within the last 7 days\.

    - **Stitch check and pyometra watch** are scheduled for event date \+ 10 and \+ 42 days\. They are skipped if that date has already passed when the record is saved\.

- **Heat episodes** \(decisions 2026\-11\-03\): owners often log each day of one heat\. A Heat record dated less than 10 days \(cats\) or 21 days \(dogs\) after the first record of the current episode is part of the same heat \(glossary\)\. It triggers nothing new\. Every rule here counts only the **first day of each episode**: the cat 2nd\-heat check, the male first\-heat check, the pyometra watch and the dog average gap\.

    - **Female dog Next date:** only the episode's first record has a Next date, and the D\-7 is planned from it\. Later records in the same episode show the episode's Next date and don't change it\. In the form, a later record shows that Next date read\-only \(decision 2026\-11\-03\), so an edit can't look as if it took effect\. **UI** \(decision 2026\-11\-04, Albar\): the read\-only field is greyed with a lock icon and a line under it, **"Mengikuti hari pertama berahi ini"** \(approved 2026\-11\-04, Albar\), so it doesn't look broken\. The type label is **"Berahi"**, replacing "Siklus Berahi" \(approved 2026\-11\-04, Albar\)\.

- **Pregnant or nursing** \(decision 2026\-11\-03\): the pyometra watch and the dog heat D\-7 are skipped if the flag is Yes on their send day \(`health_reminder_cancelled`, `reason = paused`\)\. "Go to the vet now" and "keep her away from males" don't fit a pregnant dog\.

|Pet|Reminders|
|---|---|
|Female dog, not neutered|Next date pre\-fill: \+6 months, or once 2\+ heats are logged, the average gap\. A reminder at D\-7 before the Next date\. **Pyometra watch** 42 days after the first day of each heat, unless pregnant/nursing\.|
|Female cat, not neutered|No Next date\. A 2nd heat within 60 days of the previous one triggers the cat 2nd\-heat reminder \(about spaying\), at most once per 6 months\.|
|Male, not neutered|The first heat record ever triggers the behaviour reminder, once per pet\.|
|Sex blank|No heat\-related reminders until sex is set|
|Any, Neuter record saved|**Stitch check** on day 10 after the neuter event date|
|Neutered|No heat, pyometra or spay/neuter reminders|

- **Average gap:** the mean of the gaps in days between consecutive heats, using up to the last 3 heats \(1 or 2 gaps\), added to the latest heat\. Gaps shorter than 120 days are ignored; if none are left, \+6 months is used\. The result is limited to between 4 and 12 months, and it is only a pre\-fill: the saved Next date drives the reminders\.

- **Sex changed:** heat\-related reminders are rebuilt, for future dates only\.

- **Species OTHER:** no heat, pyometra, behaviour or stitch\-check reminders\.

#### Acceptance criteria

- **AC31** A female intact dog with one heat logged on 1 Jan 2027 gets a Next date pre\-fill of 1 Jul 2027 \(\+6 months\)\. After a second heat on 1 Jul 2027 \(gap 181 days\), the pre\-fill is 29 Dec 2027 and the D\-7 reminder goes on 22 Dec 2027\. Pyometra watch reminders go on 12 Feb and 12 Aug 2027\.

- **AC32** The same dog with a third heat on 29 Dec 2027 \(gaps 181 and 181 days\) gets a Next date pre\-fill of 27 Jun 2028 and a D\-7 reminder on 20 Jun 2028\. A heat logged 20 days after another adds a gap that is ignored\.

- **AC33** A female intact cat with heats on 1 Mar and 20 Mar gets the cat 2nd\-heat reminder once\. Another heat on 10 Apr doesn't trigger a second one\.

- **AC34** A Neuter dated 3 May and saved on 3 May sends the stitch check on 13 May and cancels pending heat and pyometra reminders\. The same Neuter saved on 20 May sends no stitch check\.

- **AC35** Heat episodes and pregnancy:

    - A female intact cat with Heat records on 1, 2 and 4 Mar 2027 gets **no** cat 2nd\-heat reminder\. These are one heat; a record from 11 Mar onwards starts a new one\.

    - A cat logged every day from 1 to 7 Mar, then on 15 Mar: 1–7 Mar is one heat, and 15 Mar is a new heat within 60 days, so she gets the cat 2nd\-heat reminder \(episodes don't chain\)\.

    - A female intact dog with Heat records on 1, 3 and 6 Jan 2027 gets **one** pyometra watch, on 12 Feb 2027\. Its Next date \(1 Jul 2027\) and D\-7 come from 1 Jan only, and its average gap uses 1 Jan only\.

    - If that dog's pregnant/nursing flag is Yes on 12 Feb, the pyometra watch is not sent \(`reason = paused`\)\. The same goes for its heat D\-7 on 24 Jun 2027 if the flag is Yes then\.

### 3\.9 Delivery rules \(FR\-166\)

#### Confirmed \(2026\-10\-29\)

- **Send time:** reminders have their own daily run at **10:00 WIB** \(11:00 WITA, 12:00 WIT\), separate from the birthday push, which moves to 11:00 WIB in V1\.5\.0 \(Push and Recall PRD FR\-186\), and the anniversary push, which moves to 19:00 WIB in V1\.5\.0 \(Push and Recall PRD \(`V1.5.0/推送与召回/1-5-0推送与召回prd.md`\) FR\-182, decision 2026\-11\-10\)\. Reason: in PostHog, app opens over the 28 days to 29 Oct rise from 09:00 to a weekday peak at 11:00 WIB, and peak at 09:00–10:00 at weekends\. 10:00 catches both and leaves the day free to act\.

    - If a run fails, a re\-run the same WIB day sends that day's reminders\.

    - Reminders not sent by the end of their day are dropped\. Their D\+N points still apply\.

- **Category:** every reminder belongs to **Pet reminders** \(`PET_REMINDER`\)\. When the category is muted, the inbox item is written but no push is sent\.

- **App version** \(decision 2026\-11\-03\): reminders and launch\-queue items are planned only for users whose app last reported version **1\.5\.0 or later**\. Users on older apps get none: their app can't open the new routes or show the new types\. The Push and Recall PRD \(FR\-185\) uses the same check for its follow\-update and recall pushes; build it once and share it \(2026\-11\-10\)\.

    - When a user updates, their reminders start from the next run\. Push points already past by then are not sent \(the back\-dated rule, FR\-163\)\. Their missed suggestions go into a **personal launch queue** from the update day \(FR\-164\)\.

    - **Per device:** pushes go only to device tokens whose app reported 1\.5\.0 or later\. An old second device gets no health pushes, and its inbox leaves out notification types it doesn't know\. The user's reminders are planned if any of their devices is on 1\.5\.0 or later\.

    - Old clients keep working \(FR\-168, "Old app versions"\)\.

- **Same\-type merge:** reminders of the same type \(vaccine with vaccine, deworming with deworming, vet visit with vet visit, custom with custom\) **and the same push point** for the same pet on the same day go out as **one push**\.

    - **Different push points are never merged\.** In tiers 3–8 they are not sent as two pushes either: the pet still gets one push that day\. The priority rules below pick the winner; the other moves or is skipped under its own rule \(AC37\)\. Tiers 1–2 have no limit at all: e\.g\. one vet visit's D\-1 and another's D\-Day on the same day are both sent \(decision 2026\-11\-03\)\.

    - Why not merge them: each push point has its own copy and route\. D\-7 opens the page, while D\-Day opens the form\. One message can't say "today" and "next week" at once\.

    - **Vaccine copy:** D\-7 uses the generic D\-7 with `{vaksin}` listing the names joined with " \& "\. D\-Day uses the generic D\-Day, not "starting series"\. D\+7 uses D\+7\.

    - **Deworming copy:** D\-Day uses the Flea \+ worm copy if any External or Flea \+ worm is included, otherwise the Internal copy \(its "young" variant when FR\-169 applies\)\. D\+3 uses the external D\+3 copy \(dog or cat variant\) if any External or Flea \+ worm is included, otherwise "D\+3 internal / heartworm"\.

    - **Vet visit and Custom copy:** the normal copy\. For Custom, `{judul}` lists the names joined with " \& "\.

    - **Day counts:** if the merged reminders' Next dates differ \(one was moved\), `{hari_lagi}` uses the smallest value and `{hari}` the largest\. `{interval}` uses the largest interval among the merged reminders that have one \(e\.g\. Internal \+ Heartworm, or Internal \+ Legacy\) \(decision 2026\-11\-03\)\.

    - **Route:** a merged push opens the Health Record page with no form, because one form can't hold two records\.

    - **Analytics** \(§4\): a merged push is **one** `health_notification_sent` with `merged_count` = n\. Each reminder inside it also gets its own `health_reminder_sent`, all with the same `notification_id`\. A tap is one `health_notification_opened`\. Open and conversion rates are counted per notification, so merging never inflates or deflates them\.

- **Per\-pet daily cap: one push per pet per day**, for tiers 3–8\.

    - **Tiers 1–2 are exempt** \(decision 2026\-11\-03\): the pyometra watch, vet visit D\-1/D\-Day and custom D\-Day are always sent, even when that means more than one push for the pet that day \(rare\)\. They don't take a lower tier's slot: the best tier 3–8 reminder of that day is still sent too\.

    - **Priority** \(decision 2026\-11\-03\): pyometra watch \> **vet visit \(D\-1, D\-Day\) and custom D\-Day** \> vaccine \> deworming \> stitch check \> heat D\-7 \> check\-up \> suggestions \(including cat 2nd heat and male first heat\)\. Tiers are listed per reminder kind in Appendix C\.

        - Vet visit and custom dates rank high because a vet or the owner set them, and they can't move\. Ranking them low meant they were often lost entirely\.

    - **Within the same tier,** apply in order until one wins:

        1. A reminder that **can't move** \(D\-1, D\-Day\) beats one that can\. The loser moves instead of being lost\.

        2. The **earlier Next date** wins: the more overdue or more urgent record goes first\.

        3. Catalogue order \(Appendix C\)\.

    - Same\-type reminders with the same push point on the same day are merged, so they never compete\. Tier\-2 reminders of different types \(e\.g\. a vet visit D\-Day and a custom D\-Day\) are both sent, because tiers 1–2 are exempt from the cap\.

    - **Tier decides, not push point\.** A higher tier wins whatever its push point; e\.g\. a vaccine D\-7 beats a deworming D\-Day \(AC39\)\. Tiers 1–2 never compete; they are always sent \(AC41\)\.

    - **D\-1 and D\-Day never move\.** If one loses its slot, it's skipped\. Its D\+3/D\+7 points and the Overdue tag still apply\.

    - **Everything else moves to the next free day, for up to 3 days:** D\-7, D\+3 and D\+7 points, suggestions, check\-ups and event\-triggered reminders\. After that they are dropped\. A moved reminder only goes to a day that has no other reminder for that pet when it moves; it never displaces a reminder already there\. A day with a birthday, anniversary or first\-birthday milestone push for that pet \(defined below\) also counts as taken \(decision 2026\-11\-03; reworded 2026\-11\-10\)\. If a new reminder later lands on that day \(e\.g\. after a record is saved\), the normal priority applies, and the moved reminder can move again within its 3 days\. The launch queue \(FR\-164\) follows its own rules and is never dropped\. The pyometra watch is top priority, so it never loses its slot\.

- **No per\-user cap:** a user with 4 pets can get 4 pushes in a day, one per pet \(decision 2026\-10\-29\)\.

- **A birthday, anniversary or first\-birthday milestone push for the same pet on the same day:**

    - **What counts** \(decision 2026\-11\-10\): whether the pet gets that push that day, not when it is sent\. Birthday and first\-birthday milestone go out at 11:00 WIB from V1\.5\.0 \(Push and Recall PRD FR\-186\); the anniversary goes out at **19:00 WIB** from V1\.5\.0 \(Push and Recall PRD FR\-182\)\. All three follow from the birthday and the profile creation date, so the 10:00 run can tell in advance\.

    - **Anniversary:** only the pet that actually gets the push counts\. That is an active pet \(not read\-only\) reaching 30, 100 or 365 days; when several of the owner's pets reach an anniversary on the same day, only the one created first gets the push\. The others get an inbox item only and don't count as taken\.

    - The pyometra watch, vet visit D\-1/D\-Day and custom D\-Day are always sent, so the pet gets two pushes that day\. This is a deliberate exception to precedent C\-6 \(decision 2026\-11\-03, OQ\-9\): these are warnings or dates a vet or the owner set\.

    - Any other D\-1 or D\-Day reminder is skipped\. Anything else moves under the rules above\.

    - Milestone pushes sent when the user completes a milestone are not scheduled and don't count\.

- **Other automated pushes in V1\.5\.0** \(decision 2026\-11\-03; scope set 2026\-11\-10\): only the **new\-user recall days**, D1, D3 and D7 of the Push and Recall PRD \(FR\-183\)\. A recall day is worked out from the registration date alone, in WIB: 1, 3 and 7 days after the registration date\. This is conservative: on some of these days no recall is actually sent \(e\.g\. the user already opened the app that day\), but the day still counts\. On a recall day, tier\-8 suggestions for any of the user's pets count that day as taken and move under the rules above\. Tiers 1–7 are sent as normal: a reminder at 10:00 and a recall at 19:00 on the same day are both sent\. The launch queue already waits for any push \(FR\-164\)\.

    - **Not counted:** pushes when someone you follow posts \(Push and Recall PRD FR\-180 / FR\-181\), which are event\-driven and can't be known in advance\. Anniversaries are covered by the rule above\. The win\-back node \(`LIFECYCLE_WINBACK`\) stays off in V1\.5\.0\.

- **Pregnant / nursing:** vaccine reminders, including the first vaccine suggestion, are paused\. Deworming reminders \(D\-Day and D\+3\) are not paused and go out as normal, so nothing about them changes when the flag is cleared\.

    - **Resuming** \(decisions 2026\-11\-02 and 2026\-11\-03\)\. When the flag is cleared, by the user or by the 4\-month auto\-clear \(FR\-162\), each vaccine Next date is handled on its own\. **This table replaces the general "rebuild future points" rule \(FR\-163\) for this case\.** No point is reinstated outside it\.

        - **R** = the date of the next run after the clear \(glossary\)\. `{hari}` = the send date − the Next date\.

        - Vaccine push points are D\-7, D\-Day and D\+7 only\. Vaccines have **no D\+3**\. D\+3 is a deworming point, and deworming is never paused, so it isn't affected\.

    |    R falls on|    Last vaccine point missed during the pause|    What is sent|    Copy|    This Next date's other points|
    |---|---|---|---|---|

    - **Analytics:** the catch\-up is sent as `reminder_kind = VACCINE_D_PLUS_7` with `push_point = resume_catch_up`\.

### 3\.11 Existing records at launch \(FR\-168\)

#### Confirmed \(2026\-10\-29\)

- **Backfill:** it runs as a one\-off job at deploy \(§0\), not through the user API\. It bypasses the write rate limit and publishes no milestone events\. Existing records get a calculated Next date, shown in the list and editable\.

- **Neuter status from records:** the backfill sets neuter status to **Neutered** for every pet with a Neuter record, the same as saving one does \(FR\-162\)\. Without this, a spayed dog would still get heat and pyometra pushes\.

- **Deworming with no type** is treated as **Legacy**: Next date = event date \+ 3 months; points D\-Day \("Legacy deworming D\-Day" copy\) and D\+3 \("D\+3 internal / heartworm" copy\)\. Any typed Deworming record stops it\. Tapping opens the Deworming form with no type selected\.

- **Free\-text vaccines** are matched to a preset by case\-insensitive keywords, filtered by species:

    - **Keywords:** whole words tricat/f3, tetracat/f4, rabies, felv, kennel; any word **starting with** lepto \(lepto, leptospirosis, leptospira\) for Leptospirosis; and any word **starting with** dhpp \(dhpp, dhppi, dhppil\) for Combo DHPPi\+L\.

    - **Combo absorbs lepto:** a Combo match ignores a lepto match in the same name, since Combo includes L \("DHPPi\+L", "DHPP \+ Lepto" → Combo\)\.

    - **Two presets still matched after that** \(e\.g\. "F4 \+ Rabies"\), **or none:** no preset and no reminder until the user edits the record\.

    - **No dose is known,** so the series rule's blank\-dose case applies \(FR\-159\): under 16 weeks at the event date → \+4 weeks; otherwise \+1 year\.

    - **Display and editing** \(decision 2026\-11\-03\): a matched record shows the preset name in the list, and the edit form opens with that preset selected\. An unmatched record shows as Other with its original text, in the list and in the form\. The stored `vaccine_name` keeps the original text until the record is saved from 1\.5\.0\.

- **Heat records dated before launch** trigger nothing \(decision 2026\-10\-30\):

    - Males with a pre\-launch Heat record count as having had the behaviour reminder\.

    - The cat 2nd\-heat rule looks only at heats logged after launch\.

    - For female dogs, the Next date is still backfilled\.

- **No flood:** the back\-dated rule \(FR\-163\) applies\. A push point before the first run is never sent\. Profile\-based suggestions for existing pets go through the launch queue \(FR\-164\), never all in the first run\.

- **No Overdue wall at launch** \(decision 2026\-11\-03\): a backfilled record whose Next date is **before launch** shows **no Next and no Overdue**, and **sends no pushes at all**, including D\+3 and D\+7 points that would fall after launch \(decision 2026\-11\-03\)\. It stays quiet until the owner logs a new record in that group\. Most old Deworming records are long past their date, and a page full of "Overdue" tags on launch day would feel like blame\. Tags appear only for Next dates on or after launch, and for records saved after launch\.

- **Old app versions** \(decision 2026\-11\-03\): users on apps older than 1\.5\.0 get no reminders \(FR\-166\), but their app must keep working:

    - The API accepts saves without the new fields\. A Deworming saved without a type becomes **Legacy**, and a free\-text vaccine goes through the keyword matching above\. The record gets the default Next date, as in the backfill\.

    - An edit from an old client keeps the stored values of any new field it doesn't send \(Next date, type, preset, dose\)\. It never clears them, with two exceptions\. If the old client changes the vaccine name, the preset is re\-matched by keyword and the dose cleared\. If its new event date makes the stored Next date invalid \(on or before the event date\), the Next date is recomputed\.

    - 1\.5\.0 always writes `vaccine_name` too \(the preset label, or the "Other" text\), so old clients still show a name\.

    - The type is required only on the 1\.5\.0 form \(AC10\)\.

    - The API leaves Vet visit records out of the list for old clients, so an unknown type can't crash it or be edited into a Custom record\. Notification types they don't know are never sent to them\.

## 4\. Analytics events

**Two levels** \(decision 2026\-11\-03\)\. A **notification** is what the user actually receives: one push plus its inbox item\. A **reminder** is one planned item \(a row in `health_reminders`\)\. One notification carries one reminder, or several when they merge \(FR\-166\)\. Every rate a user experiences is counted per **notification**; every per\-kind funnel is counted per **reminder**\. They are linked by `notification_id`\.

- **`notification_kind`****:** the reminder kind whose copy row the notification uses \(Appendix C\)\. It is the reminder's own kind when unmerged\. Merged vaccines keep their shared kind\. Internal \+ External D\-Day uses `DEWORM_FLEA_WORM_D_DAY`\.

- **`push_point`**** values:** `d_minus_7`, `d_minus_1`, `d_day`, `d_plus_3`, `d_plus_7`, `resume_catch_up`, `none` \(profile\-based and event\-triggered\)\.

- **`origin`**** values:** `record`, `profile`, `event`, `launch_queue`, `resume_catch_up`\.

- **Payload:** the push payload and the inbox item carry `notification_id`, `pet_id`, `notification_kind`, `push_point`, `origin`, `merged_count`, so the app can fire its events without a lookup\.

|---|---|---|---|---|

|`health_reminder_sent`|Reminder|Backend|Once per reminder inside a notification \(so `merged_count` events per notification\)|`reminder_id`, `notification_id`, `user_id`, `pet_id`, `reminder_kind`, `push_point`, `origin`, `record_id` \(if record\-based\), `deferred_days`|

- **Attribution:** a record is `source = health_reminder` when it is saved for the same pet within **30 minutes** after a `health_notification_opened`, whatever the route\. This includes the page route of a merged or D\-7 notification, where the user opens the form themselves\. It carries that notification's `notification_id`\. If several were opened, the latest one counts\.

- **Satisfied after sending:** a reminder that was already sent can't be cancelled, so the backend stores `satisfied_at` and `satisfied_by_record_id` on its `health_reminders` row when a record satisfies or covers it\. SM\-7 reads this from the database, not from events\.

- **Idempotent:** every backend event is fired once per `notification_id` or `reminder_id`\. A same\-day re\-run \(FR\-166\) fires nothing twice\.

## 5\. Success metrics

**Method \(decision 2026\-10\-30\):** compare **90 days after launch** with **90 days before launch**, for users with at least one CAT/DOG pet\. There is no holdout\. Retention can't be measured reliably at TailTopia's size: about 8 retained users in a 10% holdout\.

Other releases in the same period will affect the numbers\. Read them alongside the change log of those releases, and alongside a descriptive comparison of users who received at least one reminder with eligible users who received none\.

The V1\.1\.0 practice applies: watch the first month, then set final targets\. The targets below are proposals\. `[ASSUMPTION]`

**Primary**

- **SM\-1 Health records logged\.** Health records created per CAT/DOG pet per month, after vs before\. Baseline: computed before launch \(AC59\); the current total is about 2 per pet that has any record, over about a year\. Proposed target: **≥ 2×**\. Validates FR\-158 to FR\-163\.

- **SM\-2 Pet\-owner monthly active rate\.** Share of users with at least one CAT/DOG pet who open the app in a calendar month, averaged over the 3 months after vs the 3 before\. Proposed target: **\+10% relative**\. Validates FR\-163, FR\-164 and FR\-167\. D30 retention of new users is tracked as **directional only**: its 0\.6% base rate is too small to read\.

**Secondary**

- **SM\-3 Reminder push open rate\.** Per notification: `health_notification_opened` \(`source=push`\) ÷ `health_notification_sent` \(`push_result=sent`\)\. A merged push counts once in both\. Break down by `notification_kind`, `push_point` and `origin`\. There is no baseline\. Proposed target: **≥ 8%**\. Validates FR\-166 and FR\-167\.

- **SM\-4 Reminder → record conversion\.** Per notification: share of opened notifications with `push_point` `d_day`, `d_plus_3`, `d_plus_7` or `resume_catch_up` that have at least one `health_record_saved` with the same `notification_id` \(30\-minute attribution, §4\)\. This includes merged notifications that open the page\. Proposed target: **≥ 30%**\. Validates FR\-167\.

- **SM\-5 Pets with any health record\.** Share of CAT/DOG pets with at least one health record\. Baseline: computed before launch\. Proposed target: **\+10 percentage points**\. Validates FR\-164\.

- **SM\-6 Next date accuracy\.** Share of records with `next_date_prefilled=true` that also have `next_date_edited=true`\. A high share means the defaults are wrong\. Monitored, with no target\.

- **SM\-7 Reminders acted on\.** Per reminder, from `health_reminders`: share of sent D\-Day, D\+3, D\+7 and catch\-up reminders with `satisfied_at` within 7 days, whether or not the push was tapped\. It catches owners who log without opening the push, which SM\-4 misses\. Monitored, with no target\.

**Counter\-metrics \(do not optimise\)**

- **SM\-C1 Pet reminders opt\-out rate\.** Share of users with a `push_category_toggled` event for `PET_REMINDER` set to off, per month\. Must not exceed **1\.5×** the pre\-launch rate\. Counterbalances SM\-3\.

- **SM\-C2 System push permission off rate\.** From `push_permission_state_reported`\. Must not rise compared with before launch\. Counterbalances SM\-2 and SM\-3\.

- **SM\-C3 Uninstalls after a reminder\.** Share of users with a `health_notification_sent` \(`push_result=sent`\) who uninstall within 48 hours, measured with AppsFlyer uninstall tracking, with FCM token invalidation as the fallback\. Counterbalances SM\-2\.

- **SM\-C4 Junk records\.** Share of `source=health_reminder` records deleted within 7 days, joined on `record_id`\. Counterbalances SM\-1 and SM\-4: it catches records logged just to silence a reminder\.

## Appendix B\. Calculation rules and health maps

The rules in §3 are binding\. Where the research maps in B\.2 and B\.3 differ from §3 or from the overrides in B\.1, §3 and B\.1 win\. The maps are kept as clinical background\.

**Naming:** the maps are quoted from the research, where the deworming row was called "Combo"; it is shown here as **Flea \+ worm**\. Everywhere else in this PRD, "Combo" means only the vaccine Combo DHPPi\+L\.

### B\.1 Overrides and additions to the research maps \(decisions 2026\-10\-29/30 and 2026\-11\-03\)

- **Series vaccines:**

    - One rule for all pets \(FR\-159\)\. Age group at the event date: **young** = under 6 months; **adult** = 6 months or older, or no birthday\.

    - Series lengths, young / adult: F3/F4 3 / 2, Combo 3 / 2, FeLV 2 / 2, Leptospirosis 2 / 2, Puppy DP 2\. Rabies, Kennel Cough, Canine influenza: 1\.

    - Dose below the length → **\+4 weeks**\. At or above the length → **\+1 year**\. Blank dose \(series presets only\): under 16 weeks at the event date → \+4 weeks, otherwise \+1 year\. Single\-dose presets \(no Dose field\) are always \+1 year\. A puppy with a Puppy DP record has a Combo series of 2\.

    - Adult first immunization = 2 doses 4 weeks apart, then yearly \(decision 2026\-11\-03\), in line with WSAVA 2024: the Leptospira part of Combo DHPPi\+L needs 2 doses, and adult cats get 2 doses of F3/F4\. This replaces the research map's kitten/puppy\-only series\.

    - **Puppy DP:** after dose 2 or a blank dose, Next date = \+3 weeks; the due vaccine is Combo dose 1 \(FR\-159\)\.

    - Rabies, Kennel Cough and Canine influenza: \+1 year\.

- **Internal deworming by age at the event date:**

    - under 8 weeks: \+14 days \(decision 2026\-11\-03; replaces the research's week 2/4/6/8 schedule, which gave gaps of 1 to 7 days\)

    - 8 weeks to 6 months: \+1 month

    - 6 months or older: \+3 months, for all cats and dogs

    - no birthday: \+3 months

- **Not used:** the "Adult goes outside", "Indoor/outdoor", "Other cats at home", "Size" and "Lifestyle" rows and columns in B\.2 and B\.3\. All dogs: senior from 7 years, spay/neuter suggestion at month 6\.

- **Heat average gap:** the mean gap in days over up to the last 3 heats, ignoring gaps under 120 days, limited to 4–12 months\. It is a pre\-fill only\.

- **Months:** calendar months with end\-of\-month clamping, always counted from the record's own event date\.

- **Other departures from the maps:** the Heat type stays available for neutered females, with a vet\-check helper \(FR\-171\)\. There is no dog spay/neuter at month 12 for large breeds, no FeLV or kennel\-cough recommendation and no Neuter info card \(§6\)\.

- `{vaksin}`: the due vaccine's name \(Combo after Puppy DP dose 2 or a blank dose\); for merged reminders, the names joined with " \& "\.

- **Merged pushes** use the copy chosen under FR\-166 "Same\-type merge" and open the Health Record page\.

- `{hari}`: today − Next date\.

- `{hari_lagi}`: Next date − today\. It is 7 on time and 4–6 when a D\-7 was moved \(FR\-166\), so a moved D\-7 never says "7 days"\.

- `{interval}`: the latest record's event date → today\. Shown in weeks under 8 weeks \(rounded down, minimum 1\), otherwise rounded to whole months\.

- **EN singular/plural:** a count of 1 uses the singular unit \("1 day", "1 week"\) in the push and the notification center \(FR\-169\)\.
